# Outside-in: what the category's decade of evidence says about coaching voices

Researched 2026-08-25 (web pass over reviews, forums, journalism, academic
work) to inform the persona brief and R2a. This file is the durable record —
[PERSONA.md](PERSONA.md) states the conclusions; this holds the evidence and,
crucially, how strong each piece is. Findings marked **[consumed]** are
already encoded in PERSONA.md or PLAN.md; the pointer says where.

**Evidence quality, stated up front:** Peloton, Down Dog, Zombies Run and
Future findings rest on multiple independent sources — treat as strong. The
famous "Freeletics' lines are repetitive" complaint is *thinner than its
reputation*: what's well-evidenced is users mourning the audio coaching's
**removal**, not a pile-on about repetitiveness. Voice-gender preference
evidence is near-nonexistent (one conjoint study on older adults). Direct
Reddit access was poor; per-app sentiment is drawn from forums, review
aggregators and journalism that quotes users.

## What reliably earns love

- **A distinct personality you can choose.** Peloton's core mechanic is
  instructor selection — "motivational mama or drill sergeant", members pick
  the ones "they think could be their friends"
  ([InsideHook](https://www.insidehook.com/wellness/people-love-peloton-instructors));
  analysts call instructors the "secret sauce"
  ([Byrdhouse](https://byrdhouse.substack.com/p/pelotons-secret-sauce)).
  Audio-only Aaptiv earns the same praise for 20+ trainer voices
  ([Garage Gym Reviews](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/aaptiv-fitness-app-review)).
  **[consumed → persona-as-data, user customization deferred]**
- **Being known by name — the strongest signal in the research.** Peloton
  milestone shout-outs: "smile from ear to ear, and push that little bit
  harder" ([Leah Ingram](https://www.leahingram.com/what-are-peloton-milestones/)).
  Future's $150/mo product is essentially "a real person notices your data"
  ([Garage Gym Reviews](https://www.garagegymreviews.com/future-app-review)).
  When Freeletics removed audio coaching, users mourned that "the Coach
  actually is my coach"; without it, "empty"
  ([forum](https://forum.freeletics.com/t/bring-back-knowledge-messages-and-motivation-audio-to-my-coach-please/2943)).
  *The perception of individualized attention is the product.* FitDuo
  genuinely has what Peloton fakes at scale. **[consumed → praise is
  data-grounded; template budget buys specificity, not adjectives]**
- **Warmth without intimidation; autonomy over commands.** Apple Fitness+
  "all inspiration, no intimidation"; NRC's Coach Bennett (calm, specific,
  lightly philosophical) earns "I thought I hated running" testimonials
  ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/11/04/1210678199/nike-run-clubs-oddly-mindful-coach));
  academic: coaches should "promote autonomy … instead of patronizing"
  ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9408500/)).
  **[consumed → warm/dry register; knowing-partner stance; R8's ask-first]**
- **Narrative continuity.** Zombies, Run! users "work out longer just to
  hear how a mission played out"; attachment to *named, voice-acted*
  characters ([Technically Well](https://technicallywell.com/zombies-run-review/)).
  **[consumed → name arrives with ears; coach references shared history]**
- **Total configurability.** Down Dog: 6 narrators plus per-session control
  of pace, length, music and *amount of instruction talk*
  ([Yogatique](https://theyogatique.com/down-dog-app-review/)). Note the
  underrated dial: not just which voice, but **how much it speaks**.
  **[consumed → voice picker v1; verbosity dial deferred with customization]**
- **A characterful persona may even be abrasive — if opt-in and funny.**
  CARROT Fit's "sadistic AI" has real fans
  ([Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/sarcastic-ai-carrot-apps-brian-mueller))
  — but "its schtick gets old fast", and teasing one member of a couple in
  front of the other has zero precedent. **[consumed → warm-dry default]**

## What reliably earns hate and churn

- **Repetition — the universal killer.** CARROT's schtick ages; Down Dog
  markets "no two sessions the same" as its core feature. A two-person
  household hears every line twice per household and quotes lines to each
  other. **[consumed → variant pools + silence, seeded pick]**
- **Robotic TTS.** *No source anywhere praises synthetic TTS delivery in
  fitness; every loved product uses recorded or live humans.* The
  category's most uncomfortable finding for this project. TTS stating true
  facts is tolerable; TTS performing sentiment is the churn quadrant.
  **[consumed → honestly-synthetic persona; short factual utterances; and
  the R2d trigger below]**
- **Talking during peak effort.** Even adored Peloton instructors: "the
  stopping to talk is extremely annoying"
  ([Peloton Forum](https://www.pelotonforum.com/threads/the-talking-is-one-thing-but-the-stopping-to-talk-is-extremely-annoying.921/)).
  **[consumed → rest is the coaching moment; mid-set is count-ins + cue]**
- **Generic advice wearing a personal costume.** Whoop Coach praised when it
  reasons from your data, dismissed when "indistinguishable from any AI
  chatbot" ([Gear Patrol](https://www.gearpatrol.com/fitness/whoop-ai-reddit-user-feedback-pros-cons/)).
  One fake-personal line poisons the real ones. **[consumed → never-list #1]**
- **Taking a loved thing away.** The Freeletics removal generated the most
  emotional language in the research. Once shipped and loved, the voice is
  load-bearing — changing it later hurts. **[consumed → persona decided
  deliberately now]**
- **Virtual coaches don't reliably move adherence** in controlled studies
  ([PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11622306/)). The voice
  is texture, not retention machinery. **[consumed → persona expectations]**

## Plan consequences beyond the persona

- **R2d loses "optional polish" status and gains a trigger.** Browser TTS is
  the likely ceiling on attachment to the voice. If the household still has
  the coach voice on after ~4 weeks of real R2a sessions, the natural-TTS
  upgrade is the highest-value polish available — schedule it then, not "if
  bored". If they mute it, R2d dies quietly and the evidence says why.
- **Milestone shout-outs are disproportionately loved** — supports the
  earned-champs pattern and suggests completion-screen milestone lines are
  higher-value than mid-session flourish. Cheap, already consistent with
  R6b.
- **This doc is the pattern for future outside-in passes** (none existed
  before 2026-08-25): findings with sources *and confidence levels*, each
  marked consumed-by or open, so conclusions never outlive their evidence.
